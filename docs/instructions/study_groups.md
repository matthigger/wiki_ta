# Study groups

- **ZPD** (zone of proximal development): help as little as possible such
  that the student succeeds.
    - Corollary: listen to students first (it'll help you diagnose where
      they've gone wrong).
- Be emotionally supportive; encourage friendships among students.
    - Every group will develop its own "feel", and it needn't be super
      intense.
    - Jokes and silliness are very welcome as long as there's regular
      attendance and meaningful effort.
- Encourage students to help each other.
    - They'll often learn better from each other (no offense, true of my
      teaching too), and it'll scale your effort to 5 struggling students at
      once.
- Don't re-teach anything.
    - You needn't present any material.
    - Minimize "whole-group" instruction; it is a waste of time for students
      who picked it up in class. Though if you suspect everybody has the same
      issue, you can offer a quick (<5 mins) intervention.
- Please do reference course materials.
    - You can earn up to 60 mins of time a week reviewing course materials
      (class notes, HWs & solutions) to refresh your memory.
- Please do not reference approaches or materials generated outside the
  course.
    - If you have something you think is worth sharing though, please ping me
      so I can take a look and we can share it more widely across everyone.
